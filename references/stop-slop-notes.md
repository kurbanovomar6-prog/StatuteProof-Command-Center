## What stop-slop Is

A skill by Hardik Pandya (hvpandya.com) for removing AI writing patterns from prose. Covers: throat-clearing openers, emphasis crutches, binary contrasts, negative listings, dramatic fragmentation, passive voice, adverbs, narrator-from-a-distance voice.

The skill has a 12-item quick-check protocol, a 5-dimension scoring table, and a 35/50 pass threshold.

## What Was Useful for StatuteProof

**The 12 quick-check rules:** adverbs, passive voice, inanimate verbs, throat-clearing openers, binary contrasts, staccato fragments, em dashes, vague declaratives, narrator distance. All adapted for StatuteProof's compliance-professional audience.

**The scoring system:** Directness, Rhythm, Trust, Authenticity, Density — each 1–10. Below 35/50: revise. Directly adopted.

**The phrase list structure:** Categories (throat-clearing, emphasis crutches, business jargon, adverbs) adapted and extended with StatuteProof-specific banned phrases.

**The structural patterns to avoid:** Binary contrasts ("not X, but Y"), negative listings, dramatic fragmentation. Applied to StatuteProof outreach and brief summaries.
