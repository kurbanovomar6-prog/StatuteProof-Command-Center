# Workflow 10: First Paid Pilot Readiness

Purpose: decide whether StatuteProof is ready for a manually activated paid Founding Pilot without implying production-scale automation.

## Required Agents

- Product Manager: confirms pilot scope, buyer value, and boundaries.
- Source Monitor: confirms source pack and remediation state.
- Evidence Trail: confirms proof-backed records exist for demo/source claims.
- Risk + Brief Pipeline: reviews any brief shown to the pilot.
- QA / Critic: checks broken flows, billing wording, and sample/demo labels.
- Legal Language: blocks legal advice, certification, and guarantee claims.

## Readiness Criteria

1. Pilot scope is explicit and limited.
2. Active plan is manually approved; plan request does not equal paid activation.
3. Source readiness truth is current and conservative.
4. DFSA/remediation sources are disclosed clearly.
5. At least one proof-backed sample brief exists and is demo-labeled.
6. No fake Stripe/payment success exists.
7. Customer-facing copy uses monitoring intelligence and not legal advice language.
8. Operational runbook exists for onboarding, source readiness review, and weekly review cadence.
9. Tests and validators pass for touched areas.
10. Founder/operator approval is recorded before activating any paid pilot.

## Output

Produce a pilot readiness memo:

- ready yes/no
- exact pilot offer
- sources included/excluded
- evidence available
- manual activation steps
- customer caveats
- next blocker
