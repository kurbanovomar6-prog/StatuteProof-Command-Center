1. Overview

What this sample covers

StatuteProof checks whether official regulatory sources can be monitored reliably, what extraction method is likely to work, and where access or source complexity may limit source monitoring. For a UAE fintech profile, the first source set usually focuses on banking and payments supervision, securities or virtual asset perimeter questions, AML/CFT, business regulation and tax updates.

5. Pilot recommendation

Recommended Founding Pilot scope

For a payments or fintech team, a Founding Pilot would usually begin with one regulatory profile and a focused official-source set, then expand only after the source profile and alert workflow prove useful.

Founding Pilot would monitor

  • 1 regulatory profile
  • 5-8 confirmed official sources
  • Weekly regulatory brief
  • Email or Telegram alerts
  • Source proof links

Known limitations to confirm

  • Exact source paths and publication pages
  • Restricted or low-content pages
  • PDF-heavy source behavior
  • Topic filters for payments, AML/CFT, tax or VASP scope
  • Client review workflow and alert frequency
