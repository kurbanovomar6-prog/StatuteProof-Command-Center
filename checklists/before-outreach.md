# Checklist: Before Outreach

Complete all items before sending any StatuteProof outreach message.

## Evidence Grounding

- [ ] The monitoring capability referenced in the message is real (not fabricated)
- [ ] If the message references a detected change: a real run_id exists for it in source_runs.jsonl
- [ ] No claim is made that cannot be traced to a real evidence record or pipeline capability

## Legal Safety

- [ ] Message does not contain any forbidden phrase from docs/forbidden-phrases-reference.md
- [ ] No guarantee claim ("guarantee compliance", "prevent fines", "never miss")
- [ ] No regulator affiliation claim ("official partner of VARA", "certified by DFSA")
- [ ] No replacement claim ("replace your MLRO", "no need for a compliance officer")
- [ ] No 100% accuracy or completeness claim
- [ ] Short disclaimer present in email footer (not required for LinkedIn opener if no product claim)

## ICP Fit

- [ ] Lead is in a UAE-regulated firm with a documented obligation to monitor the covered regulators
- [ ] Contact role is CCO, MLRO, Head of Compliance, or in-house Counsel
- [ ] Message references a specific regulatory obligation or recent regulatory event for this lead
- [ ] Message leads with the lead's situation, not with the product

## Anti-Slop

- [ ] No adverbs
- [ ] No passive voice ("updates are monitored" → who monitors them?)
- [ ] No throat-clearing openers
- [ ] No binary contrasts
- [ ] No generic superlatives (seamlessly, comprehensive, robust, game-changer)
- [ ] "You/your" dominates over "I/we/our"
- [ ] Under 150 words (email) or 80 words (LinkedIn)
- [ ] One CTA only

## Review Complete

- [ ] `#anti-slop-writing-review` score >= 35/50
- [ ] `#marketing-outreach-review` decision is SEND or REVISE (not BLOCK)
- [ ] Any REVISE issues fixed before sending
