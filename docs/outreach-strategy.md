# Outreach Strategy

## Purpose
Define safe, concise B2B outreach for StatuteProof.
Outreach must be specific, source-backed, low hype, and legally safe.

## Core Offer Definition
Tier 1: Free source readiness review.
A 20-minute review of which official-source pages the prospect tracks and whether evidence capture is audit-ready.

Tier 2: 2-week pilot.
Monitor two selected official sources for two weeks and provide human-reviewed source health notes.

Tier 3: 30-day pilot.
Monitor two official sources for 30 days, provide weekly summaries, evidence records, and reviewed briefs for meaningful changes.

## Message Format Rules
LinkedIn first touch: 300 characters or fewer.
Cold email: 150 words or fewer.
Follow-up 1: 80 words or fewer.
Follow-up 2: 100 words or fewer.
Pilot offer: 150 words or fewer unless prospect asks for detail.
Use one CTA per message.

## Personalization Rules
Allowed sources: official regulator pages, company website, public license/register pages, public press releases, public LinkedIn profile details, public job title.
Disallowed sources: private personal data, guessed email, unverifiable claims, scraped hidden data, fake posts, fake events.
If a personalization fact cannot be verified, do not use it.

## Sequence Schedule
Day 0: first message with one verified hook and one CTA.
Day 6: follow-up with one new specific detail or useful question.
Day 14: final follow-up or graceful close.
Maximum: 3 messages per lead before pause.

## Six Outreach Angles
1. VARA source readiness: Hook: SAMPLE / FAKE VARA source pack review. Offer: free readiness check.
2. CBUAE circular monitoring: Hook: payment firms may track circulars manually. Offer: 2-week pilot.
3. Evidence trail gap: Hook: official-source proof is hard to assemble after the fact. Offer: evidence record sample.
4. Consultant leverage: Hook: consultants monitor for multiple clients. Offer: monitoring infrastructure review.
5. AML team workflow: Hook: AML-related updates need fast internal triage. Offer: brief format review.
6. Founder compliance load: Hook: regulated startup founders often carry compliance operations early. Offer: source list check.

## Objection Handling
Objection: We already use counsel.
Response: That makes sense. StatuteProof is not counsel; it can support the monitoring/evidence layer your counsel may review.

Objection: We check sources manually.
Response: That is exactly the workflow we map in a source readiness review: which pages, what cadence, and what evidence is retained.

Objection: We do not need another tool.
Response: Understood. The free review can stay as a checklist, not a software commitment.

Objection: Send details.
Response: Send a one-page scope with two-source pilot, weekly summary, and evidence record fields.

Objection: Too early.
Response: Ask whether to reconnect after a license milestone or source pack is defined.

Objection: Is this legal advice?
Response: No. It is monitoring information and evidence capture for review by qualified legal/compliance professionals.

## Pre-Send Checklist
1. Lead is qualified by ICP + Lead Research.
2. Personalization has source URL.
3. Message stays within channel limit.
4. One CTA only.
5. No forbidden claim.
6. No fake regulatory event.
7. Short disclaimer included where appropriate.
8. Legal Language and QA / Critic review completed for batches.
