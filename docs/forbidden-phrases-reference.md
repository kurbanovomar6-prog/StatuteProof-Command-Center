# Forbidden Phrases Reference

## Purpose
This reference helps Legal Language, Outreach Writer, Risk + Brief Pipeline, and QA / Critic identify unsafe product language. Forbidden phrases may appear here only as examples of what not to say.

## Unsafe Phrase Table
| Unsafe Phrase | Why Unsafe | Approved Replacement | Risk Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| AI lawyer | Implies legal services and lawyer replacement | Official-source regulatory monitoring tool | High | Never describe the product or AI as a lawyer. |
| we guarantee compliance | Creates a guarantee and warranty-like claim | Supports compliance review with evidence-backed monitoring | High | Guarantees are prohibited. |
| guarantee compliance | Same guarantee risk even without pronoun | Supports compliance teams with official-source monitoring | High | Use support language only. |
| prevent fines | Claims outcome control over regulator action | Helps teams identify updates faster for review | High | Do not promise fine prevention. |
| never miss a change | Perfect monitoring claim cannot be supported | Monitors selected official sources on a defined schedule | High | Always qualify source scope. |
| always up to date | Implies perfect timeliness and completeness | Shows last checked timestamp and detected changes | High | Use timestamped evidence. |
| certified by [regulator] | Implies official endorsement | Built for monitoring public official sources | High | Only say certified if actually documented. |
| official partner of CBUAE / VARA / DFSA | False affiliation risk | Not affiliated with regulators; monitors public official sources | High | No partnership implication. |
| 100% accurate | False precision and overconfidence | Evidence-backed output with human review gates | High | Accuracy claims require proof and still should be qualified. |
| automated compliance decisions | Implies replacing human compliance judgment | Automated monitoring support with human review before decisions | High | Decisions remain with humans. |
| replace your legal team | Direct replacement claim | Supports legal and compliance teams with evidence records | High | Never position as replacement. |
| our AI will catch every update | Perfect capture claim | Designed to detect changes on selected monitored sources | High | Use selected-source language. |
| sleep easy knowing you are compliant | Implied guarantee and emotional assurance | Keep a clearer evidence trail for compliance review | Medium | Avoid emotional certainty. |
| we handle compliance for you | Implies outsourcing compliance responsibility | We help monitor official sources and prepare review briefs | High | Customer remains responsible. |
| no need to manually check regulators | Overstates automation and discourages verification | Reduce manual checking burden with monitored source alerts | Medium | Do not tell teams to stop verifying. |
| compliance without legal review | Encourages bypassing counsel | Briefs are for review by qualified legal or compliance professionals | High | Human review required. |
| automatic regulatory decisions | Implies automated legal/compliance decisioning | Structured monitoring outputs for human regulatory review | High | Decisions must remain human. |
| avoid all penalties | Outcome guarantee | Identify relevant updates earlier for internal review | High | Avoid penalty language. |
| regulator-approved monitoring | False authority if not documented | Monitoring public official-source updates | High | No approval claim. |

## Usage Rules
- Use the approved replacement phrase exactly when possible.
- If a phrase implies legal advice, regulator affiliation, guaranteed outcome, or replacement of professionals, treat it as High risk.
- If the phrase is not exact but the implication is similar, escalate to Legal Language Agent.
- Client-facing briefs require the full standard disclaimer.
- Outreach may use the short disclaimer only when space is constrained.
- All forbidden phrase decisions remain governed by evidence-first review, explicit owner, and human approval when risk is high or evidence is incomplete.
