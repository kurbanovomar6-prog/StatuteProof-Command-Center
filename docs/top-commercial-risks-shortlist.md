# Top Commercial Risks Shortlist

Date: 2026-06-16

1. **False confidence**: a buyer might think StatuteProof guarantees regulatory completeness. It does not.
2. **CBUAE concentration**: 43.5% of readiness-supported sources are CBUAE, which weakens broad UAE positioning.
3. **Incomplete MLRO workflow**: Acknowledge & Assess is still spec-only, so the product is not yet a complete audit workflow.
4. **Parser maintenance burden**: source-specific adapters will need ongoing care as regulator sites change.
5. **False positives from hash drift**: the held DFSA source proves this risk is real.
6. **Weak VARA/DIFC coverage**: this limits VASP and DIFC buyer fit.
7. **Sample/live confusion**: frontend/demo areas must clearly separate real proof-backed records from sample data.
8. **Support load**: early customers will need founder-operated setup and source review.
9. **Enterprise competitor mismatch**: StatuteProof cannot pretend to match Thomson Reuters/Wolters Kluwer/CUBE breadth.
10. **Pricing trust**: $399 is only fair if positioned as a focused UAE monitor with proof and human review, not as an enterprise replacement.
