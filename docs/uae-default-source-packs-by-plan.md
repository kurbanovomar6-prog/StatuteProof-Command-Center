# UAE Default Source Packs By Plan

## 1. Principle

Source packs should map to buyer trust and activation readiness, not raw URL volume. StatuteProof can offer strong default expertise while still being honest that each source needs extraction quality, evidence, and baseline checks before active monitoring.

Current public truth remains:

**13 enabled UAE sources; 9 readiness-supported; 4 under extraction remediation.**

## 2. Internal Demo Pack

Target size: 5-10 sources.

Purpose:

- Show parser/evidence capabilities across official HTML, JavaScript-rendered pages, selectors, PDF/listing risk, hashes, diffs, and sample brief review.
- Keep the demo honest by including remediation states.

Recommended categories:

- VARA enforcement or rulebook candidate.
- DFSA rulebook candidate, marked preview/baseline pending.
- UAE FIU publication/circular source, with remediation warning if generic extraction is weak.
- CBUAE regulations, with access/source-specific caveat.
- Existing proof-backed sample brief source.

Activation requirement:

- No customer delivery.
- Demo labels only.
- Evidence paths shown only where proof exists.

Safe wording:

“Internal demo pack showing source readiness, evidence, and remediation states.”

## 3. Free Source Readiness Review

Target size:

- 1 custom public source submitted by the prospect.
- Recommended UAE source map based on the customer profile.

Purpose:

- Lead generation and trust.
- Show how Source Lab tests public official sources and explains why a source is or is not ready.

Included:

- URL safety check.
- No-save preview.
- Extraction quality.
- Failure/remediation reason.
- Suggested activation path.

Not included:

- Active monitoring.
- Legal advice.
- Guaranteed parsing.
- Evidence-confirmed claims unless a saved proof run is explicitly performed.

Safe wording:

“We test whether your public source can be monitored safely and explain extraction readiness.”

## 4. Founding Pilot - $199/month

Target size:

- Around 20 curated endpoints tailored to the customer licence.

Example packs:

- VASP: VARA + UAE FIU + relevant CBUAE + sanctions/TFS + selected legislation.
- DIFC firm: DFSA + DIFC + UAE FIU + sanctions/TFS + selected federal sources.
- ADGM firm: ADGM/FSRA + UAE FIU + sanctions/TFS + selected federal sources.
- Consultant: mixed top-20 map based on client portfolio.

Activation requirements:

- Manual activation after source readiness review.
- No-save validation for included sources.
- Saved proof/baseline for sources shown as evidence-backed.
- Human/legal-safe review before customer-facing briefs.

Safe wording:

“Manual pilot source pack activated after readiness review.”

## 5. UAE Monitor - $399/month

Target size:

- 40-60 official endpoints after validation.

Purpose:

- Professional UAE compliance monitoring baseline across VARA, UAE FIU, CBUAE, DFSA/DIFC, ADGM/FSRA, SCA, sanctions/TFS, federal legislation, and selected tax/compliance sources.

Requirements before marketing 40+:

- Top-40 no-save validation report.
- Source-specific selectors/adapters for recurring nav-shell failures.
- Saved evidence/baseline for activated sources.
- Source readiness counts generated from canonical logic.
- UI copy reviewed by Source Monitor, Evidence Trail, QA, and Legal Language gates.

Requirements before marketing 60:

- Top-60 no-save validation report.
- Remediation backlog separated from active pack.
- No “validated” status unless evidence/proof criteria justify evidence-specific wording.
- Source health/operational-risk states surfaced.

Safe wording after validation:

“40+ official UAE source endpoints mapped and validated through readiness checks, with remediation status shown where extraction needs work.”

Unsafe wording:

“60 validated sources.”

## 6. Consultant / Enterprise

Target size:

- 100+ endpoints eventually, including client-specific public sources.

Purpose:

- Multi-client, custom source onboarding, audit exports, source readiness reviews, API/GRC export, and white-label or consultant workflows.

Requirements:

- Multi-tenant source ownership.
- Stronger custom-source storage controls.
- Source baseline history.
- Evidence export/audit records.
- Acknowledge & Assess workflow.
- Manual review and customer-specific activation approvals.

Safe wording:

“Custom official-source coverage and evidence-backed monitoring workflows for consultant-managed portfolios.”

## 7. What Is Not Included By Default

- Private portals.
- Login/CAPTCHA/paywall sources.
- Law firm commentary.
- Social media.
- News sites.
- Non-UAE regulators unless separately scoped.
- Legal advice.
- Guaranteed compliance.

- a 60-candidate official UAE source map under validation;
- a realistic top-40 validation path;

It should not yet market 40+ or 60 active/validated sources.
