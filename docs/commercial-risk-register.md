# Commercial Risk Register

Date: 2026-06-16

| Risk | Severity | Likelihood | Impact | Why It Matters | Current Mitigation | Missing Mitigation | Next Build | Customer-Safe Wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alert fatigue / false positives | P1 | Medium | High | Buyers will churn if updates are noisy or not material. | Noise/source-health gates and held DFSA drift source. | Materiality filters and MLRO assessment workflow. | Acknowledge & Assess plus alert recommendation states. | "We separate source-health issues from regulatory update review." |
| False negatives / missed updates | P0 | Medium | High | A missed regulatory update damages trust. | Conservative source health and remediation statuses. | Stability reports, source-health timeline, manual fallback instructions. | 30-day source stability report. | "Monitoring may be affected by site changes; limitations are shown." |
| Parser/source-health maintenance burden | P1 | High | High | UAE regulator sites change often; a solo founder can get buried. | Source-specific adapters and validators. | Maintenance SLA/process. | Source-health dashboard and weekly operator runbook. | "We disclose remediation and quality drops." |
| CBUAE concentration | P1 | High | Medium | 43.5% CBUAE share weakens "balanced UAE Monitor" story. | Truthful distribution report. | More VARA/DIFC/ADGM alternate coverage. | VARA PDF + DIFC listing/table adapters. | "Strong CBUAE/payments coverage; VARA/DIFC expansion in progress." |
| VARA/DIFC/ADGM coverage gaps | P1 | High | High for VASP/DIFC buyers | A VASP or DIFC buyer may not see enough coverage. | Some VARA/DFSA/ADGM sources exist. | Direct VARA PDFs, DIFC sources, ADGM alternate components. | Diversification sprint. | "Coverage is source-specific; unsupported sources are visible." |
| Hash drift / unstable pages | P1 | Medium | High | Drift can create fake change alerts. | `AE-dfsa-aml-ctf-sanctions` held. | Deterministic path alignment and drift diagnostics. | DFSA deterministic hash fix. | "We hold unstable sources instead of alerting on them." |
| Source access blocking | P1 | Medium | Medium | Some official sites block or change access. | Access-blocked classification. | Better operator remediation and fallback policies. | Source-health timeline and manual check queue. | "Access limitations are reported, not hidden." |
| Customer overexpectation | P0 | High | High | Buyers may assume legal coverage/completeness. | Legal-safe copy and forbidden claims. | Onboarding acceptance and limitation checklist. | Pilot onboarding/legal acknowledgement. | "Monitoring intelligence only. Not legal advice." |
| Legal advice / liability | P0 | Medium | High | Regulated buyers are sensitive to unauthorized advice. | Disclaimers and legal-safe reviews. | Persistent acknowledgement and counsel-review boundary. | Registration/pilot agreement acknowledgement. | "We support review; we do not determine legal obligations." |
| Trust risk if demo overclaims | P1 | Medium | High | One inflated demo claim can kill credibility. | Demo script with caveats. | Live/sample banners and API-backed source table. | Demo/live mode indicator. | "Sample/demo where sample; proof-backed where proof exists." |
| Pricing risk | P2 | Medium | Medium | $399 may feel high before workflow is implemented. | $199 controlled pilot available. | Stronger Acknowledge & Assess workflow and balanced coverage. | Keep $399 as limited early-access. | "$399 is for monitored source pack + review workflow as it matures." |
| Support burden | P1 | High | Medium | Early customers will need founder help. | Founder-led pilot framing. | Clear SLA and source remediation process. | Manual operator playbook. | "Founding pilots are deliberately hands-on." |
| Solo-founder operational risk | P1 | High | High | Source maintenance, sales, support, and product all depend on one person. | Validators and docs. | Repeatable ops checklist and automation. | One-command validation + weekly source health report. | "We are in controlled pilot, not broad self-serve rollout." |
| Competitor comparison risk | P2 | Medium | Medium | Enterprise tools have breadth, analysts, integrations, history. | Narrow UAE-first positioning. | Better proof of niche value. | Case studies from pilots. | "Narrower and more transparent, not enterprise replacement." |
| Security/privacy risk | P1 | Medium | High | Compliance buyers will ask about data handling. | Product mostly monitors public sources. | Trust/security page and retention controls. | Security/trust page and data retention policy. | "We monitor public sources; customer notes need access controls." |
| Evidence/audit workflow gap | P1 | High | High | Without Acknowledge & Assess, buyer gets detection but not review closure. | Spec and demo artifact. | Real review model, export, locked records. | Implement MVP workflow. | "Audit workflow is next; current demo is sample." |
| "Just RSS reader" risk | P1 | Medium | High | If evidence/diff/workflow is not visible, buyer compares to cheap tools. | Proof paths, hashes, gates, source-health states. | UI that makes evidence and assessment obvious. | Evidence page + Acknowledge & Assess. | "The value is evidence-backed review, not only change detection." |
