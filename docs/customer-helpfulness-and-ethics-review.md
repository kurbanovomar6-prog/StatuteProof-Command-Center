# Customer Helpfulness And Ethics Review

Date: 2026-06-16

## Does StatuteProof Reduce Customer Risk?

Yes, in a narrow but real way: it reduces the chance that a small compliance team forgets to check important official sources and gives them evidence of what was monitored.

It does not remove regulatory responsibility, legal interpretation needs, or the need to verify official source material.

## Where It Could Create False Confidence

- If "62 readiness-supported" is heard as "complete UAE compliance coverage."
- If customers assume no alert means no regulatory change.
- If sample/demo data is mistaken for live proof.
- If source-health failures are hidden.
- If Acknowledge & Assess is marketed before implementation.

## Honest Limitation Language

"StatuteProof monitors selected public official or officially linked sources that are technically accessible and permitted to monitor. Monitoring may be affected by website changes, access restrictions, parser quality, publication delays, and source structure changes. Monitoring intelligence only. Not legal advice."

## How To Prevent Legal-Advice Reliance

- Require onboarding acknowledgement.
- Put "not legal advice" on evidence, brief, and export screens.
- Separate evidence from interpretation.
- Require human review before customer delivery.
- Never auto-assign legal obligations.

## What Every Customer Should Understand Before Paying

- Source scope is finite and disclosed.
- Some sources are under remediation.
- StatuteProof does not guarantee all changes are captured.
- Customers remain responsible for compliance decisions.
- Evidence is support for review, not legal conclusion.

## Responsible To Sell Today

- Founder-led $199 pilot.
- Narrow $399 early-access monitor for matching CBUAE/AML/payments buyers.
- Source readiness review.
- Manual proof-backed monitoring summaries.

## Irresponsible To Sell Today

- Enterprise-grade compliance automation.
- Legal advice or obligation mapping.
- Complete UAE regulatory coverage.
- Guaranteed monitoring.
- Self-serve product that implies all workflows are fully implemented.
