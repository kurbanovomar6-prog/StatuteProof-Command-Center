# Would I Buy This? Honest Test

Date: 2026-06-16

## Skeptical MLRO

- Would I buy? Partial.
- At what price? $199 pilot if my sources match; $399 only with review workflow or very relevant source pack.
- What builds trust? Proof paths, hashes, source-health status, held-source examples, no overclaims.
- What makes me say no? "62 sources" without explaining distribution and limitations.
- Biggest worry: false confidence or noisy alerts.

## Compliance Consultant

- Would I buy? Yes, for a pilot.
- At what price? $199-$399 if I can use the evidence in client work with proper disclaimers.
- What builds trust? Exportable audit record and source readiness report.
- What makes me say no? No multi-client workflow and sample/live confusion.
- Biggest worry: support burden and client-specific customization.

## Small VASP Founder

- Would I buy? Maybe.
- At what price? $199; $399 feels hard unless VARA coverage improves.
- What builds trust? VARA rulebook/PDF monitoring and simple alerts.
- What makes me say no? Too much CBUAE, not enough VARA.
- Biggest worry: paying for sources I do not care about.

## DFSA Compliance Officer

- Would I buy? Maybe, if DFSA-specific pack is shown.
- At what price? $199 pilot; $399 after DIFC/DFSA drift issues are resolved.
- What builds trust? DFSA AML/rulebook/notices with stable evidence and held drift transparency.
- What makes me say no? DIFC remediation gaps.
- Biggest worry: source stability.

## Enterprise Procurement / Legal Team

- Would I buy? Not yet.
- At what price? Custom proof-of-concept only.
- What builds trust? Security docs, data retention, roles, audit export, references, SLA.
- What makes me say no? Solo-founder risk and missing enterprise controls.
- Biggest worry: liability and operational continuity.

## Final Buyer Test

If I were the buyer, I would pay **$199/month today** for a controlled, transparent, founder-led pilot if the source scope matches my regulated activity.

I would pay **$399/month today** only if I am a CBUAE/AML/payments-heavy buyer and I get founder support plus clear limitations. I would not pay $399 for a broad self-serve UAE monitor yet.

The proof that would change my mind: 30-day stability report, implemented Acknowledge & Assess, API-backed source status UI, and stronger VARA/DIFC coverage.
