# Pricing Value Analysis

Date: 2026-06-16

## Verdict

| Offer | Verdict | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free Source Readiness Review | Justified | Good trust-builder and diagnostic wedge. |
| Founding Pilot $199/month | Justified for controlled pilots | Fair if founder-operated, source scope is explicit, and customer understands limitations. |
| UAE Monitor $399/month | Partially justified | Fair for CBUAE/AML/payments-heavy buyers who value proof-backed monitoring; premature for broad UAE positioning until Acknowledge & Assess and diversification improve. |
| Consultant/Enterprise custom | Partially justified | Only for manual/high-touch engagements, not self-serve enterprise. |

## What $199 Must Include

- Source readiness review for the buyer's relevant UAE source pack.
- A small agreed source scope, not all possible sources.
- Weekly or periodic monitoring summary.
- Evidence/proof paths for monitored changes.
- Clear limitation/remediation report.
- Founder/operator support.
- "Monitoring intelligence only. Not legal advice."

## What $399 Must Include

- The full readiness-supported UAE source pack where relevant to the buyer.
- Source-health status and remediation transparency.
- Evidence-backed diffs/proof hashes.
- Manual review before customer-facing conclusions.
- Prioritized alert/review queue.
- At least a lightweight Acknowledge & Assess workflow or a manual equivalent.

## What Should Remain Custom

- Enterprise multi-team workflows.
- Customer-specific source packs.
- Consultant multi-client workspaces.
- Legal/policy mapping.
- Guaranteed response/SLA.
- Integration with GRC, email, Slack/Teams, or ticketing.

## Should $399 Wait?

It should not be sold as broad self-serve "UAE Monitor" yet. It can be sold as a limited early-access UAE Monitor to a buyer whose pain matches current strengths: CBUAE, AML/CFT, DFSA, ADGM/FSRA, and payments/open-finance monitoring.

It should wait for Acknowledge & Assess before being pushed as the main commercial plan.

It should wait for more VARA/DIFC balance before being pushed to VASPs or DIFC-heavy firms.

## ROI Argument

The honest ROI is not "we guarantee compliance." It is:

- reduce manual official-source checking;
- preserve evidence of what was monitored;
- make broken sources visible instead of silent;
- speed up internal review by showing source, hash, diff, and proof path;
- help small compliance teams avoid missing obvious official-source updates.

## Why Pay Instead Of Manual Monitoring?

Manual monitoring is cheap until it is forgotten, undocumented, or inconsistent. StatuteProof's value is the repeatable evidence trail and source-health transparency. A compliance team can show what was checked, when, and what happened next.

## Who Is Not A Good Fit

- Enterprise bank expecting global regulatory intelligence.
- Buyer expecting legal advice or obligation interpretation.
- Buyer needing production integrations immediately.
- Buyer whose core sources are mostly VARA/DIFC and not yet covered deeply enough.
- Buyer unwilling to accept public-source access limitations.

## Who Is A Very Good Fit

- UAE fintech/payment firm tracking CBUAE, AML/CFT, open finance, payment token, consumer protection, and FIU/EOCN updates.
- ADGM/DFSA-regulated smaller firm needing evidence-backed monitoring but not enterprise tooling.
- Compliance consultant who wants a source-readiness and evidence trail workflow for UAE clients, with manual review.

## First Five Paid Customers Should Receive

- Founder-led onboarding.
- Explicit source scope.
- Monthly source health report.
- Manual review of first alerts.
- Access to proof-backed demo/audit sample.
- Clear limitation list.
- No self-serve automation promises.

## Underpriced If

- Acknowledge & Assess is implemented.
- Source-health timeline and audit export exist.
- VARA/DIFC coverage improves.
- Customer-specific source packs are maintained reliably.

## Overpriced If

- Buyer only sees generic source count.
- Acknowledge & Assess remains missing.
- Demo relies on sample/mock data.
- Buyer needs legal interpretation, global coverage, or enterprise controls.

## Recommended Pricing Copy

Founding Pilot:

"$199/month for a founder-led UAE official-source monitoring pilot. Includes source readiness review, agreed source scope, evidence-backed monitoring summaries, and visible remediation status. Monitoring intelligence only. Not legal advice."

UAE Monitor:

"$399/month early-access UAE Monitor for firms whose priority sources are in the readiness-supported pack. Includes proof-backed source monitoring, source-health visibility, and review support. Broader VARA/DIFC coverage and Acknowledge & Assess workflow are in active buildout."
