# MLRO Homepage Review

## Purpose
Evaluate whether the homepage makes a UAE MLRO understand the monitoring pain, proof value, and next step quickly.

## When to use
Use for homepage review, landing page rewrite, CTA review, demo page review, or source readiness offer positioning.

## When not to use
Do not use for backend source logic, legal opinions, or broad GTM research unrelated to the page.

## Required inputs
- Homepage files, screenshot, URL, or copy.
- Target segment if narrower than UAE MLRO/CCO.

## Step-by-step procedure
1. Check first viewport: who it is for, what it monitors, and what proof is shown.
2. Check whether evidence/diff/audit trail appears before generic feature claims.
3. Check source coverage clarity and limitations.
4. Check CTA strength: Source Readiness Review or founding pilot.
5. Check dashboard screenshots for SAMPLE labels if not live.
6. Check legal safety and absence of certainty claims.
7. Recommend one highest-impact page change.

## Output format
- Score /10.
- Conversion blockers.
- Proof gaps.
- Unsafe copy if any.
- Single highest-impact change.

## Safety rules
- Do not overstate coverage.
- Do not hide limitations.
- Do not present mock data as live monitoring.

## StatuteProof-specific constraints
The homepage should sell evidence-backed monitoring, not generic AI SaaS.
