# Legal-Safe Copy Review

## Purpose
Block unsafe product language and rewrite claims into calibrated monitoring language.

## When to use
Use before publishing website copy, briefs, sample reports, outreach, pricing pages, demo screens, or CTAs.

## When not to use
Do not use to provide legal advice or decide regulatory obligations.

## Required inputs
- Copy to review.
- Intended audience and placement.
- Whether copy is public website, sample brief, dashboard, or outreach.

## Step-by-step procedure
1. Identify product claims, legal claims, compliance outcome claims, regulator-affiliation claims, and AI autonomy claims.
2. Block language that presents StatuteProof as legal counsel, a compliance guarantee, a regulator-certified service, or a replacement for professionals.
3. Check that samples are clearly labeled SAMPLE / DEMO / NOT CUSTOMER DATA.
4. Require human-review language near brief/risk outputs.
5. Replace broad certainty with evidence-backed monitoring language.
6. Add or verify a disclaimer where needed.

## Output format
- SAFE / HOLD.
- Unsafe lines.
- Why each line is risky.
- Replacement copy.
- Disclaimer requirement.

## Safety rules
- Do not draft legal advice.
- Do not imply official regulator partnership.
- Do not imply complete coverage or guaranteed outcomes.

## StatuteProof-specific constraints
Approved framing: official-source monitoring, evidence-backed compliance briefs, supports compliance review, human-reviewed, not legal advice, audit-ready evidence trail, source readiness review.

## Example invocation
"Use legal-safe-copy-review on this homepage hero before launch."
