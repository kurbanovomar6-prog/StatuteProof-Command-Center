# Anti-Slop B2B Copy

## Purpose
Turn vague SaaS copy into specific compliance-operations language for UAE MLROs and CCOs.

## When to use
Use for homepage copy, outreach, product descriptions, pilot offers, dashboard text, and founder updates.

## When not to use
Do not use to invent product capabilities, legal advice, or unsupported claims.

## Required inputs
- Draft copy.
- Target reader.
- Placement and desired action.
- Proof available for any concrete claim.

## Step-by-step procedure
1. Remove vague phrases, filler, hype, and generic AI language.
2. Replace abstract benefits with source, evidence, diff, review, and audit-trail specifics.
3. Prefer calm verbs: monitor, detect, compare, record, review, export.
4. Keep limitations visible near coverage claims.
5. Make CTA concrete: source readiness review, pilot workspace, evidence dry run.
6. Check legal-safe boundaries.

## Output format
- Diagnosis.
- Rewrite.
- Claims that require proof.
- Legal-safety notes.

## Safety rules
- Do not add unsupported coverage numbers.
- Do not imply automated legal decisions.
- Do not erase limitation language.

## StatuteProof-specific constraints
Every strong marketing claim should connect to an evidence artifact or a human-review workflow.

## Example invocation
"Use anti-slop-b2b-copy to rewrite this pilot email for a VARA MLRO."
